Application information

What the form asks you to prepare.

The current application workflow collects the following information. This is a guide to the form, not a complete admissions eligibility checklist.

PROGRAMME CHOICES

Choose up to three

Select a first-choice programme and study format. Second and third choices are optional in the form.

SPONSOR DETAILS

Provide sponsor information

The programme-choice step asks for a sponsor’s name and relationship to the applicant.

EDUCATION HISTORY

Record prior study

The education step asks for institution, programme pursued, and start and end dates.

EXAM INFORMATION

Enter examination details

The form lists WASSCE and SSSCE, May/June (School) and Nov/Dec (Private) sittings, and fields for index number and year.

EXAM RESULTS

Add subjects and grades

Results are entered by subject and grade. The current form lists grades A1 through F9.

PASSPORT PHOTOGRAPH

Upload a photo

The admissions workflow includes a required passport photograph upload.

!

Confirm current requirements before applying

VTIU may update programmes and admissions procedures. The official admissions portal and admissions team are the source of truth for eligibility, documents, fees, and dates.
