Use this checklist to guide your questions. It does not replace the official programme requirements.

01 / PROGRAMME

Know what you want to study

Confirm the programme name, award, duration, and current availability with VTIU.

02 / DOCUMENTS

Confirm what to provide

Ask which academic records, identity documents, and supporting information are required.

03 / TIMING

Check dates and costs
